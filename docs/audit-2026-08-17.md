## 2. Still to fix — prioritized

### CRITICAL (mobile usability, easy wins)
1. **Touch targets < 48×48px** (10+ found) — increase mobile menu toggle, close-menu, and language buttons to min 48×48 with padding.
2. **0px gaps between Flights/Buses/Tours/Insurance strip buttons** — add ≥8px gap to the ServiceIcons strip container (flex gap).
3. **`touch-action: manipulation` missing site-wide** — add to buttons/links in `globals.css` (removes 300ms tap delay).

### CRITICAL (legal — content)
4. **GDPR: privacy policy missing required clauses** — add data subject rights, lawful basis for processing, DPO/contact, data retention periods, data transfer + third-party sharing disclosures. Needs business contact info.
5. **User rights portal** — page/form for data access, deletion, opt-out requests.

### MEDIUM (SEO)
6. **Title 49 chars → 50-60** — e.g. `A9 Global Travels & Tours | Luxury Travel in Myanmar` (53).
7. **Meta description 146 → 150-158 chars** — lead with IATA / since 2015, end with CTA.
8. **Schema: add LocalBusiness + ContactPoint + FAQPage** JSON-LD (currently TravelAgency only). FAQ page content exists for FAQPage schema.
9. **Home content thin (144 words) + 'travel' density 5.06%** — expand home copy to 400-600 words with semantic variants (journey, tours, expedition…). Needs business copy.
10. **Images: 8/18 missing width/height; WebP 6%** — where raw `<img>` is used, move to `next/image` (auto width/height + WebP via optimizer) or add dims; convert local jpgs to WebP.
11. **Form inputs at 13px font** → set ≥16px on mobile (prevents iOS auto-zoom).

### LOW / optional
